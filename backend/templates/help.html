1. Health Facility Web Registration

Registering your health clinic or dispensary on the AfyaGPT web portal

4-Step Registration Wizard Overview
Step 1Facility Basics

Enter official Facility/Clinic Name, Official Email Address (used for Admin login), and Country (default Kenya).

Step 2Mandatory Location Access

County, Sub-County, Address & Browser GPS Location Grant. Manual typing of coordinates is disabled; GPS permission is required to enable the Next button.

Step 3Contacts & Web Presence

Facility Contact Phone. Official Website, Facebook, and Twitter/X URLs are explicitly marked as (Optional).

Step 4Security & Account Activation

Create facility admin password. If core fields and location access are provided, the account activates instantly (is_draft = False).

Location Permission Requirement

To ensure accurate mapping of health centers on the interactive Kenya GPS map, browser location permission is mandatory. Click "Grant Permission & Capture GPS" on Step 2. If browser GPS delays, click "Permission Granted? Click Here to Enable Next Button" to proceed.

2. Facility Admin & Operational Dashboard

Managing staff, triage data, and DHIS2 HMIS indicator reports

Dashboard Navigation Categories
  • Main Overview DHIS2 aligned indicators, total pediatric registrations, triage breakdown (Green/Yellow/Red), and KEPI immunization rate.
  • User & Role Controls Approve pending CHW & Nurse registration requests, toggle account active status, change roles, and trigger 6-digit PIN resets.
  • Clinical Operations & GPS Map View live interactive CHW home visit markers, registered facility roster, and export patient CSV reports.
  • HMIS & DHIS2 Reporting Export Kenya Ministry of Health standardized reports (MOH-711, MOH-710, Triage CSV, Patient Registry).
Real-Time Streaming & Tab Persistence

The web dashboard polls light delta updates every 10 seconds without refreshing the page. Refreshing your browser preserves your active tab (e.g. #patients, #users) automatically.

3. Android Mobile Application Operations

Field guidelines for Community Health Promoters (CHPs) and Clinicians

Pre-Login Facility Search

App launch fetches all active facilities and caches them locally. Health workers search and select their facility during signup; County is populated automatically.

6-Boxed PIN Security

Sign in securely using your phone number/email and a 6-digit PIN with individual boxed digit indicators and a cursor line.

Split DOB Patient Registration

Date of Birth is entered via 3 distinct dropdowns (Year, Month, Day) to prevent format errors and accurately calculate under-5 pediatric age.

Bilingual Support (English & Swahili)

Switch between English and Kiswahili in Settings for Community Health Promoters (CHPs) operating in rural Kenya units.

5. Offline Resilience & Cloud Sync

How AfyaGPT functions seamlessly in remote locations without internet

AfyaGPT is engineered with an Offline-First Architecture. All patient registrations, triage assessments, and vaccination records are saved locally in the device's Room SQLite database.

Offline Mode

Full triage assessment and patient registration operate completely offline using local WHO IMCI rules.

Auto-Sync on Connectivity

When network connection is detected, clicking "Sync Now" uploads pending offline records to the cloud and downloads facility patient updates.
