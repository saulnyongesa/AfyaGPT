About AfyaGPT

Bridging the Frontline Healthcare Gap in Rural Clinics

In many health posts across Kenya, frontline health workers face high patient loads and zero internet connectivity. AfyaGPT brings WHO IMCI guidelines to life through structured algorithms, automated respiratory rate timing, and offline AI decision support.

Strict WHO IMCI & Kenya MoH Compliance

Follows exact pediatric triage algorithms for danger signs, respiratory infections, diarrhea, malaria, and severe malnutrition.

Zero-Downtime Room SQLite Engine

All patient profiles, triage history, and KEPI vaccine schedules are saved locally on disk and synced seamlessly when online.

Core Features

Designed for Field Clinical Excellence

WHO IMCI Triage Engine

Structured 5-step triage covering Danger Signs, Vitals 60s Timer, Respiratory, Diarrhea, Malaria, Ear Problems, and MUAC Nutrition.

AfyaGPT AI Assistant

Persistent AI decision support linked to patient profiles, offering first-dose antibiotic dosing and emergency referral criteria.

KEPI Vaccine Tracker

Auto-generates the official 14-dose Kenya Expanded Programme on Immunization schedule upon newborn registration.

124+ Offline Protocols

Instant access to clinical treatment protocols for Pneumonia, Malaria ACTs, Diarrhea Plans A/B/C, SAM, and Neonatal Sepsis.
